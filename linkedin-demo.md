“Hi everyone! For Task 1 of my internship with @SWYNEX Technologies, I defined StudyFlow, a student productivity app concept.”

- Keep the video around 60–90 seconds.
- Show the screens and one main journey: Home → Add Task → Save → Tasks.
- Use readable zoom and slow cursor movement.
- Close unrelated tabs and notifications.
- Do not claim backend or features work unless they actually do.
